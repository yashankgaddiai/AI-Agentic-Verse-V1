FAQ

Questions, answered

How fast will my first post go live?+

Seven days after we receive your footage or AI avatar files. If we miss it, your first month is free.

What do I need to send you?+

Real You: short clips filmed on your phone. AI Avatar: one clear HD video of yourself (5 minutes) and one voice recording (10 to 15 minutes) of you talking naturally. You send the avatar files once.

Will the AI avatar look and sound like me?+

That is the point of the build. We make your avatar from your own video and voice, and you approve a test video before anything goes live. If it does not look and sound like you, you do not pay.

Can I start with my own footage and switch later?+

Yes. Most clients start as Real You and move to the AI avatar once they see the content working. You can switch any month with no penalty.

What is the difference between the two engines?+

The Content Engine covers content production only: scripts, strategy, editing, captions, and repurposing. The Full Growth Engine adds higher volume, full social media management, and paid ad management, with one report for content and ads together.

What do you mean by done for you and done with you?+

Done for you means our team handles the agreed production work. Done with you means we set up the workflows, help you hire and train an editor, and teach you or your team to run them.

Which platforms do you cover?+

We work out which platforms matter for you on the strategy call, then build content for each one.

How do I see results?+

You get a weekly performance report. On the Full Growth Engine, it covers content and ad performance together.

Why only four clients a quarter?+

The senior team you meet on the call is the team that runs your account, and every client gets our full attention.

How much does it cost?+

It depends on the engine, the volume, and whether you choose done for you or done with you. We cover it on the strategy call, and you leave with a written game plan either way.
